Tweaks

Smoother frames and lighter memory, a pseudo V-Sync readout, progressive editor copy/paste — plus a telemetry panel while you drive and in replays (suspension, grip, the car's angle to its travel, checkpoint times against your ghosts), Trackmania-style checkpoint splits, a key to hide or show your ghosts mid-run, a statistics tracker, shareable clips of your runs, player profiles and world-record z-scores on the leaderboard, practice tools (any speed, until any time on the track, and savestates) while you test a track you are building, a free camera in the garage, and a collaborative editor where several people build one track together and watch each other test drive it.
